StageLight guide

Let other apps trigger StageLight

Other apps on the show computer or the show network (LiveStage, a sound desk app, camera control, a Stream Deck, QLab…) can GO cues and fire actions in StageLight by sending it short OSC messages over UDP.

Safe by design. A trigger can GO, stop, clear, black out or set the master, but it can never arm physical output or change the patch. Cues only reach the real lights while you have armed output in StageLight.

1. Turn it on

  1. Open ☰ → Network & triggers.
  2. Under Allow other apps to trigger, choose who may send:
    • This Mac only: apps running on this computer. This is the default.
    • Show network: other computers too. Type their IP addresses, separated by commas (for example 192.168.1.20, 192.168.1.31), or * for any device. Messages from anyone else are ignored and counted as rejected.
  3. Leave the port at 1461 unless something else uses it, then press Save. The top line should read Listening on …:1461.

2. Point the other app at StageLight

In the other app, add an OSC (UDP) output to this computer's IP address (or 127.0.0.1 on the same Mac), port 1461. Arguments are strings, whole numbers or decimals.

3. Use named triggers (recommended)

Instead of hard-coding cue numbers in the other app, send a name, and decide in StageLight what that name does. You can change the action later without touching the other app.

  1. In Named triggers, type a name such as chorus, pick an action (GO a page or a specific cue, back, stop, clear, blackout, master level, LED wall, play a cue) and press Add trigger.
  2. Press the ▶ button next to it to try it from StageLight.
  3. In the other app, send /stagelight/trigger "chorus".

Messages StageLight understands

Pages and cues can be given by name, by number (1 = first) or by ID.

AddressArgumentsDoes
/stagelight/triggernameFire a named trigger
/stagelight/go[page]GO the next cue (on that page, or the one running)
/stagelight/go/cuepage, cueGO a specific cue, e.g. "Act 1" 4
/stagelight/backPrevious cue
/stagelight/stop[fade seconds]Stop and fade to dark
/stagelight/clear[fade seconds]Release manual changes back to the cue
/stagelight/blackout1 or 0Blackout on or off
/stagelight/master0 to 1Master level
/stagelight/playcue [fade]Play a library cue outside the pages
/stagelight/ledwallmedia or offChange the LED wall preview
/stagelight/ping[reply port]Answers /stagelight/hello with the version, whether output is armed, and the running page and cue
/stagelight/catalog[reply port]Answers with every page, cue, named trigger and library cue, so the other app can show them in a list to pick from

If something is wrong (an unknown trigger, a page that does not exist), StageLight answers /stagelight/error with the reason. The full list is also under Addresses StageLight understands in the sheet.

Telling other apps what StageLight is doing

Under Send to, add the other app as a target (its IP and OSC port, or an HTTP address). Tick Announce and it hears /stagelight/cue/live with the page and cue every time a cue GOes. A cue can also send a look summary (colour temperature, level and colour), for example to keep a camera's white balance right: open the cue in the cue list and choose When this cue GOes, send. Use Ping and Send look to test a target.

Look Link: colour temperature for cameras

A camera or lighting app can follow the stage's colour per zone. Under Look Link zones, pick one or more groups (or type fixture numbers), give the zone a role and add it. Face / key is the light cameras white-balance to; front, back, top and side are context; effects and audience lights are usually ignored. Each zone reports its colour temperature (Kelvin), tint (Duv: plus is greenish, minus is magenta), level, and whether it is white light a camera should balance to or a coloured look it should leave alone. The numbers come from what the fixtures are told to make, not from a meter. A 200W or 7R on a wheel position nobody has named yet counts as unknown.

Press Look on a target to choose which zones it gets and when: Only cue sends (just the cues where you add a look send), Every GO, or GO + live changes (also when you move faders, run effects, change master or blackout; at most four times a second, with a final look when things settle). Preview shows exactly what would be sent; Send now sends it. In a cue's When this cue GOes, send, Preset in that app recalls one of the other app's own presets (pick from the list it published, or type its id); it is sent just before the look. The other app's replies appear next to each message in Recent messages. Full contract for the other app's builder: docs/LOOK-LINK.md.

Checking it works

  • Recent messages at the bottom of the sheet lists everything received and sent, who sent it, what StageLight did and how long it took.
  • Send /stagelight/ping from the other app first. If nothing shows up, check the IP address and port in the other app, and that its computer is on the allowed list.
  • A message marked rejected came from a device that is not allowed: add its IP, or choose This Mac only if both apps are on this computer.
