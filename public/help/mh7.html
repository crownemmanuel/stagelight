Before physical control

Proposed routes: Hog 1 ← raw Art-Net 0; Hog 6 ← raw Art-Net 5. Apply Input mappings on Processor 1 and yield the corresponding channels. Disarm the app, enter matching routes in Connection & diagnostics, save and re-arm. New routes are initially blank; MH7s remain preview-only until configured. Identify left/right before running effects.

Pan/tilt use 540°/250°. Position alignment, axis reversal and joystick are available. The profile opens only a steady shutter; lamp control is available through the Strike lamp and Douse lamp buttons (Look tab). Reset remains unavailable. Beam/Spot mode is available in Look and via fixture.set values.opticalMode (beam or spot). Rotating-gobo selection is not implemented yet.

Verified intensity setup

Open beam is the default: dimmer channels 1/2, shutter 255 on channel 3, beam mode 0 on channel 5, open gobo 0 on channel 8. Confirmed by the operator on all seven universe 6 heads. Intensity controls, cues and MCP share this default. Spot mode remains unverified on this rig; universe 1 heads still need separate diagnosis. Lamp strike is a separate command.

Lamp strike and douse

Channel 21: strike range 40–59 (app sends 50), douse range 60–79 (app sends 70). Commands run for five seconds, then return to neutral 0. Five seconds is the app pulse duration, not a timing requirement stated by the manual. They require live output and only affect selected MH7s. API/MCP: fixture.lamp / lights_fixture_lamp, with ids and action strike or douse. Commands are transient and are not saved in looks or replayed after restart. Lamp ignition is not acknowledged by Art-Net.

Yield the full 21-channel footprint on Hog, including the control channel. The manual recommends at least five minutes warm-up before lamp-off, then leave fixture power on for cooling. A lamp cannot restart above 45°C. For dousing, enable Off via DMX on the fixture. Do not confuse lamp-off with intensity blackout.
